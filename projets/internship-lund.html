Research Internship — Lund University, Sweden

Technician intern, April – June 2025 · Department of Physical Geography and Ecosystem Science

Context

The internship took place at Lund University within the Department of Physical Geography and Ecosystem Science. The research question was: how does the management of forests and agricultural land influence the climate?

My role and field work

I took part in the installation of environmental sensors and carried out measurement campaigns on both forest and agricultural sites.

À compléter : matériel utilisé (type de capteurs, gazomètres, stations...), sites exacts, protocole de pose.

Data processing

I developed a Python program for the automated processing of CO2 and CH4 flux data, then analysed and interpreted five months of measurements.

À compléter : bibliothèques utilisées (NumPy, Matplotlib...), chaîne de traitement, volume de données.

What I learned

À compléter : compétences techniques, travail en équipe international, lien avec ton orientation hydraulique/environnement.
